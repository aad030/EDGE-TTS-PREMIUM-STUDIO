# 🎙️ Edge TTS Premium Studio 

An advanced, high-retention AI Voiceover Studio designed for professional content creators and video editors. This tool eliminates the hassle of manually splitting audio clips by automatically slicing your **English** or **Urdu** narrative scripts into clean, sequential, sentence-by-sentence MP3 audio tracks.

Built with **Streamlit** and powered by **Microsoft Edge's Neural TTS Engine**.

---

## 🔥 Key Features

* **⚡ Automated Sentence Splitting:** Smart regex engine detects punctuation markers for both English (`.`, `!`, `?`) and Urdu (`۔`, `؟`) to generate separate tracks.
* **🎭 Multi-Voice Studio Integration:** Pre-loaded with top-tier, high-retention cinematic voice models:
    * `Asad (Urdu - Male)` - Perfect for Urdu documentaries and deep narration.
    * `Uzma (Urdu - Female)` - Ideal for emotional storytelling or poetic quotes.
    * `Christopher (English - Male)` - Ultra-premium cinematic voice for high retention.
    * `Ava (English - Female)` - Exceptional tone for engaging presentations and facts.
* **🎛️ Audio Fine-Tuning:** Dynamic sliders to adjust **Speed (%)** and **Pitch (Hz)** on the fly.
* **📦 Timeline-Ready Bulk Export:** Download all generated audio clips bundled neatly into a single **ZIP file** with sequential naming (`track_001.mp3`, `track_002.mp3`), ready to drop straight into CapCut or Adobe Premiere Pro.
* **💎 UI Dashboard:** Features a modern, glassmorphic dark-theme design tailored for a premium SaaS feel.
